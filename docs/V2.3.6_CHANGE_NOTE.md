# v2.3.6 change note

- Replaces one-shot sequential/concurrent calibration with repeated measurements and median selection.
- Alternates protocol order by repeat to reduce order and thermal-drift bias.
- Detects an initial near-tie using a configurable relative median margin (default 2%) and adds two adaptive tie-break measurements per protocol.
- Selects the lower final median after tie-breaking, without imposing a preferred mode.
- Preserves the v2.3.5 scalar calibration fields while adding full samples, medians, gap, tie-break activity, selected mode, and selection reason.
- Adds a focused crossover matrix at scale `1e18` with widths `1.25e11`, `1.50e11`, `1.75e11`, and `2.00e11`.
- Emits crossover aggregates separately from the full ten-case matrix.
- Expands regression coverage from 8 to 15 tests.
- Preserves the Gold1 implementation files byte-for-byte from v2.3.4/v2.3.5.

The 2% near-tie margin is an instrument-stability policy, not a mathematical constant. Cross-machine claims still require exact validation and matched benchmark settings.
